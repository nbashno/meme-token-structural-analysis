WAR Arena — Privacy Policy

Version 2026-01-v1

1. What we collect. (a) A Telegram identity provided by Telegram when you open the Mini App (a numeric user id, and optionally username/first name/language), transmitted as signed initData. (b) Your usage: scans, monitors, and analytics requests you make. (c) Payment records: transaction ids, rail (Stars/TON), amounts, and balance/entitlement history. We do not collect your Telegram password, contacts, or messages.

2. What we do NOT collect. We do not take custody of your funds, private keys, or seed phrases. TON payments are signed in your own wallet. We never ask for a private key.

3. Third parties. Token data comes from GMGN; opening the app and Stars payments involve Telegram; TON payments involve the TON blockchain (public and permanent by nature). Your use is also subject to their terms and privacy practices. We share only what is necessary to provide the Service (e.g. a token address to query GMGN).

4. Why we process it. To authenticate you, deliver scans/monitoring, process payments and credit, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Legal bases where applicable: performance of contract and legitimate interest.

5. Retention. We keep account, payment, and usage records as long as your account exists and as required for legal, tax, and anti-fraud purposes, then delete or anonymize them.

6. Your rights. Subject to your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, and withdraw consent. Requests: contact @Bashmaz on Telegram.

7. Security. Secrets are never logged; initData signatures are verified; payments are idempotent. No system is perfectly secure — you use the Service at your own risk.

8. Children. The Service is not for anyone under the legal age in their jurisdiction.

9. Changes. Material changes re-prompt for consent.

Contact: @Bashmaz on Telegram.
